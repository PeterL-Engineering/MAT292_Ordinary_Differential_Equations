\subsection{Handling Stiffness & Stability}
\label{subsec:handling-stiffness-and-stability}

The H-H equations are considered stiff ordinary differential equations.
This feature is characterized by the slow-varying solutions resulting in small stability regions.
Accordingly, explicit methods such as the three methods we implemented, are generally inefficient for solving this set of ODEs.

In general, these theoretical properties were illustrated in the minimum step size ratio for convergence as shown in ~\ref{tab:min_steps}.
As expected, the fourth order Runge-Kutta method achieved a much lower step ratio for convergence compared to the others.
Nonetheless, improved Euler's method required a higher ratio than Euler's method.
