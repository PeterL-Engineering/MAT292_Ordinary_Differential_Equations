Applying rigorous LTE analysis to 4th order Runge-Kutta Method (RK4) for Hodgkin-Huxley requires the vector Taylor expansion:

\[
\mathbf{y}(t_{n+1}) = \mathbf{y}(t_n) + h\mathbf{f}(t_n, \mathbf{y}_n) + \frac{h^2}{2}\mathbf{f}'(t_n, \mathbf{y}_n) + \frac{h^3}{6}\mathbf{f}''(t_n, \mathbf{y}_n) + \frac{h^4}{24}\mathbf{f}'''(t_n, \mathbf{y}_n) + \mathcal{O}(h^5)
\]

where the total derivatives involve the Jacobian $\mathbf{J} = \frac{\partial \mathbf{f}}{\partial \mathbf{y}}$:

\[
\mathbf{f}' = \mathbf{f}_t + \mathbf{J}\mathbf{f}
\]
\[
\mathbf{f}'' = \mathbf{f}_{tt} + 2\mathbf{J}\mathbf{f}_t + \mathbf{J}^2\mathbf{f} + \frac{\partial \mathbf{J}}{\partial \mathbf{y}}\mathbf{f}\mathbf{f}
\]

The RK4 method is designed to match the Taylor expansion up to $h^4$:

\[
\mathbf{y}_{n+1}^{RK4} = \mathbf{y}_n + h\mathbf{f} + \frac{h^2}{2}\mathbf{f}' + \frac{h^3}{6}\mathbf{f}'' + \frac{h^4}{24}\mathbf{f}''' + \mathcal{O}(h^5)
\]

The LTE emerges from the $h^5$ term mismatch:

\[
\text{LTE} = \mathbf{y}(t_{n+1}) - \mathbf{y}_{n+1}^{RK4} = -\frac{h^5}{720}\mathbf{f}^{(4)}(\xi_n) + \mathcal{O}(h^6)
\]

For Hodgkin-Huxley, the complex nonlinear couplings mean $\mathbf{f}^{(4)}$ involves high-order derivatives of the ionic current functions and gating variable kinetics. Nevertheless, the asymptotic order $\mathcal{O}(h^5)$ is preserved.
Thus RK4 remains fourth-order for Hodgkin-Huxley, though the LTE constant depends on the full nonlinear structure through the Jacobian and higher derivatives.
